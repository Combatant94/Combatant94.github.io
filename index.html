01 / FLAGSHIP

UK Business Distress Monitor

Where is corporate distress really concentrated — not just where raw failures are highest?

220,650 records272/272 industries validated3.56 baseline MAE

What looks like a simple insolvency count hides a harder question: which industries are genuinely under the most pressure once business population is considered? I combined Insolvency Service records with ONS business counts, validated the published totals before analysis, compared raw volume with population-adjusted risk, and tested forecasting models against a simple baseline.

02 / HEALTHCARE

NHS Waiting Times

A large headline waiting-list number does not show where pressure is concentrated. I transformed 77M+ raw RTT rows into a Python → SQL → Power BI workflow, then used provider and waiting-band views to make variation, long waits and data-quality issues easier to investigate.

03 / RETAIL

UK Retail: 1988—2025

How has British retail changed across recessions, the pandemic and the shift online? I analysed more than 35 years of official ONS retail data to separate long-term growth from seasonality, examine the rise of internet sales and build forecasts designed for business interpretation rather than just model accuracy.

04 / ECONOMY

UK Economic Payment Network

Instead of viewing industries as isolated rows in a table, I treated almost £4 trillion of UK inter-industry payments as a network. Centrality and clustering reveal which sectors act as economic connectors, where payment relationships concentrate and how the structure changes over time.

Which airports matter because of where they sit in the global network, not simply because they are busy? My MSc research modelled 3,214 airports and 66,771 routes as a distance-weighted network and compared multiple centrality measures to uncover very different definitions of a “hub”.
